# 3 — Own-seat dock: the player's seat leaves the ring

**Legacy:** was #35 in the love-letter effort.

**What to build:** rework the ticket 1 tabletop so the viewer's own seat lives **only** in the bottom dock — the ring's redundant self tile is deleted and the opponents take the freed space. The dock becomes a full seat (name / tokens / badges / own discard pile) above the hand. Opponents arrange in a symmetric top row (2p one top-center, 3p two corners, 4p three across) with enlarged tiles (~18–20rem, same info, bigger text). The center table shrinks (deck stack ~2.2rem, burned + 2p face-ups smaller); seat discard piles pack tighter (same readable thumbs, more overlap, one-row wrap). Choices become **tap-the-seat** (legal target tiles light up; a thin hint line replaces the choice panel; the Guard's second step is a compact name-chip row; "X is choosing…" stays as a thin muted line). Playing becomes **on-card**: the hand card's top-right rank badge (love-letter-client/09) is deleted and a small **Play chip** appears there on the selected card (regret intact — tapping another card or the card again switches/deselects; phone fallback: swap the bottom name-caption for "Play" if the corner chip proves too small). The dock carries the seat's animation hooks so card scenes keep working.

**Blocked by:** none — reworks the ticket 1 ring and dock, the love-letter-client/11 play bar, the love-letter-client/09 rank badge, and the love-letter-story/04 scene anchoring in place.

## Comments

**Grilling session (2025) — full decision record:**
- **Q1 (core move)** — the viewer's own seat leaves the ring; the bottom dock is the viewer's full seat: name ("You"), ♥ tokens, protected/out badges, own discard pile, hand, turn banner. *(The dock must carry the pile — it is public deduction info and the scene-animation landing target.)*
- **Q2 (arrangement)** — opponents in a symmetric top row: 2p one top-center, 3p two corners, 4p three across. Freed space grows the tiles, not the table.
- **Q3 (piles)** — the center table shrinks (deck/burned/face-ups); seat discard piles keep readable card faces but pack tighter (overlap + one-row wrap). Shrinking the thumbs was explicitly rejected — the piles are the at-a-glance deduction surface.
- **Q4 (choices)** — tap-the-seat: legal targets light; a thin hint line in the slot replaces the panel; the Guard's second step is compact name chips; "X is choosing…" stays as a thin line. Choices remain un-cancelable.
- **Q5 (play)** — on-card "Play" chip on the selected card; regret survives (tap elsewhere / again to switch or deselect); the full-width bar is deleted.
- **Q6 (phones)** — one layout, scaled; band scroll is the documented last resort, now rarer (3 tiles max).
- **Q7 (choosing line)** — keep "X is choosing…" as a thin muted line for everyone.
- **Rank badge (follow-up)** — the top-right `.rank-badge` (love-letter-client/09) is deleted; the card art carries the rank numeral top-left (love-letter-client/09's pixel analysis: "the stylized numeral sits top-left, exactly under the old pill position"; author-confirmed legible at hand size).
